Culture and everyday life

Japan combines traditional customs with modern life. You can visit a temple in the morning and explore a busy shopping street in the evening.

Japanese food includes sushi, ramen, and many regional dishes. Tea ceremonies and seasonal festivals are also part of the country's culture.

When visiting, follow local signs, keep your voice low in quiet spaces, and ask before taking photos of people.
